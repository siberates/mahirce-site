Questions

What office owners usually ask.

Does our phone number change?

No. You keep your number. Missed calls simply get a text reply from your office name.

Will it work with our scheduling software?

In most cases, yes. On the demo we look at what you use today and I tell you honestly what is possible.

What if a patient asks something it can't answer?

It passes the conversation to your front desk, so a real person takes over. Nothing urgent gets lost.

Is there a long contract?

No. It's month to month, and you can stop any time.

Do we have to change our website?

No. Booking and the website assistant can be added to the site you already have.

How long does setup take?

Usually a few days after the setup form. You approve the messages before anything goes live.
